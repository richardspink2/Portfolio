About

Trades Experience Moving Into Construction Technology

My name is Richard Spink. I am a software development student at the College of Western Idaho with a background in pipefitting, rig welding, demolition crew leadership with Clean Harbors, personal training, and hands-on field work.

I first became interested in coding through games and modding communities, but my professional path also taught me how real work gets done: crews coordinating under pressure, problems changing in the field, safety staying front of mind, and communication making or breaking a job.

That is why I am especially interested in construction technology. I want to work on software and support systems that help field teams, contractors, and project teams solve practical problems. I am building skills in Python, SQL, C#, HTML, CSS, JavaScript, databases, and version control while looking for roles where my trades and crew-lead background are assets.

What I Bring

Field Awareness

I understand the pace, constraints, and communication style of jobsite work.

Technical Learning

I am actively building software, database, web, and C# projects through coursework and practice.

Customer Fit

I can connect with field users because I know what it feels like to solve practical problems with tools in hand.
