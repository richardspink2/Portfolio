About me

Building useful software, one project at a time.

Software development started as an interest and quickly became the work I wanted to pursue. Now, as a student at the College of Western Idaho, that interest has grown into full-stack applications built for real businesses and everyday users.

My approach

Most projects begin with a simple question: what does the person using this actually need? From there, the work covers responsive interfaces, authentication, databases, testing, and deployment. Careful debugging and clear documentation help keep every feature understandable and dependable.

Previous experience in welding, pipe fitting, personal training, and content creation still shapes that approach. Those jobs required precision, communication, consistency, and close attention to the people depending on the final result.
